Privacy Policy for Conveyor Jam

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Conveyor Jam ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you play our mobile puzzle game on iOS and Android.

1. Information We Collect

Conveyor Jam does not require account registration and does not directly collect or store your name, email address, or personal profile on our own servers.

Your game progress (current level, unlocked levels, stars, lives, power-up counts, and sound/theme preferences) is stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Conveyor Jam displays rewarded and interstitial advertisements using Google AdMob. Google AdMob and its partners may automatically collect and process certain device and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads, including:

  • Device or advertising identifiers (such as Apple IDFA or Google Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • App and ad interaction events and crash/performance diagnostics

You can review how Google uses this data in Google's Privacy Policy .

3. Your Choices & Controls

  • iOS App Tracking Transparency: You can allow or deny ad tracking at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android Ad Settings: You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

4. Children's Privacy

Conveyor Jam is intended for a general audience (13+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository: github.com/vidapacifica9/conveyer-jam .
